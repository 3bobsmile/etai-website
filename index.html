Estimated Time for AI

Know how long your AI will take before you hit send.

ET.Ai predicts how long Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other models will take to answer a prompt, then updates the estimate live while the answer is being written.

The problem

You type a prompt, press send and wait. Will it be 5 seconds or 5 minutes? Today's AI tools don't say, so people stare at a spinner instead of deciding whether to wait, switch tasks or pick a faster model.

How it works

Total time is time to the first word, plus thinking time, plus writing time. ET.Ai estimates each part.

1

Type your prompt

ET.Ai reads what you're asking for, from a one-line answer to a long coding task, and predicts how long the answer will be.

2

See your estimate

Before you send, you get a range such as "15–40 seconds, most likely 22", based on measured speeds for each model.

3

Watch the live countdown

Once the answer starts, ET.Ai measures the real speed and updates the time left, like a download bar.

Features

Built for people who send a lot of prompts every day.

Estimate before you send

A clear time range for the model you picked.

Live countdown

Real-time progress while the answer is written.

Faster-model tips

See when another model would answer in a fraction of the time.

Cost estimate

Token cost shown next to the time.

Ready notifications

Switch tabs and get pinged when a long answer is done.

Accuracy tracker

See how close past estimates were, so you know how far to trust them.

The waiting game

Five seconds or five minutes?

You send a prompt and the spinner starts. With no sense of the wait, it’s hard to know whether to stay put, switch tasks, or choose a faster model.

✓Know what kind of wait to expect
✓Decide when to switch tasks
✓Choose a model that fits your moment
✓See the ETA move as the answer arrives

Simple by design

An ETA for every prompt.

ET.Ai turns the unknown wait into a useful estimate, then learns from the real response as it comes in.

01 / PROMPT
⌕

Tell it what you need

From a quick question to a long report or coding task, the request gives the estimate its starting point.

02 / PREDICT
◷

See the likely wait

Get a time range before you send, informed by the model, likely answer length, and task complexity.

03 / UPDATE
↗

Track it as it writes

Once the response begins, ET.Ai uses the actual pace to refresh your time remaining.
